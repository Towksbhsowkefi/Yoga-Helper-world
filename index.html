About Still & Steady
Yoga can be a practice of attention, not perfection.

Still & Steady offers general educational information about yoga and mindful movement. Listen to your body, adapt movements to your abilities, and consult an appropriately qualified professional for individual guidance, especially if you have an injury or medical concern.
